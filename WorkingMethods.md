# Working methods section

12 Hemigrapsus Oregonensis were collected and distributed into four groups: one ambient temperature (cold) group without the presence of predators (Carcinus maenas), one warm temperature condition treatment without the presence of predators, one ambient treatment with a predator present, and one warm temperature condition with a predator present. C. Maenas were kept seperate by mesh fencing.

Frozen fish was cut into twelve chunks, thawed, and weighed for initial mass*. 





*Note: fish was not soaked in water before massing, so post-feeding trial weights increased despite visual confirmation of feeding. Standardization will be attempted.
